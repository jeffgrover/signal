Recording gaps and interpretation notes

Gaps are missing observations, not proven internet outages. The intervals below are longer than two hours, measured on the recorded local clock. Smaller gaps and clock changes are not included.

Two redundant Pi-hole resolvers are available for comparison, but these records do not identify which resolver handled a test. DNS failures are not automatically WAN failures. Timestamps have no stored timezone offsets; client latency methods differ. Test averages are sample averages, not time-weighted service guarantees. The archive is a snapshot, not a live monitor.
